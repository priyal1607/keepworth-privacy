# Privacy Policy — Keepworth

_Last updated: [DATE]_

## The short version

Keepworth does not collect any data. There is no account, no sign-in, no
analytics, no advertising and no network code in the app at all.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — item names, prices, purchase dates, categories, notes
and your history of logged uses — is written to a single file inside the
app's own private storage on your device.

That file is never transmitted anywhere. It is not uploaded, synced, shared
or backed up to any server operated by the developer, because the app has no
server and makes no network requests.

## What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

- No personal information
- No contact details
- No location data
- No device identifiers
- No usage or analytics data
- No crash reports beyond what Apple collects if you have opted in to share
  diagnostics with developers at the system level

## Third parties

Keepworth contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks and no
analytics frameworks.

## Your device backup

If you have iPhone backups enabled (to iCloud or to a computer), Keepworth's
data file is included in that backup, in the same way as data from any other
app. That backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own backup
settings, not by this app.

## Exporting your data

Settings → Export Data as JSON produces a readable copy of everything you
have entered and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. What happens to
that file afterwards is entirely under your control.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Delete All Data removes everything immediately. Deleting the app
from your device also removes all of its data.

## Children

Keepworth is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone,
including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects anything, this policy will be
updated before that version is released, and the App Store privacy details
will be updated to match.
